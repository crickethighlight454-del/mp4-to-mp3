Why ToolBox

Every tool here processes your files using your own device's browser engine — nothing is ever sent to a remote server. That means your files stay private, there's no upload wait, and once you install ToolBox as an app, most tools keep working even without an internet connection. New tools are added regularly, and each one gets its own dedicated page with clear instructions, so the toolbox grows without getting more confusing to use.
